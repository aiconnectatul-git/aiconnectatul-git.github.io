Install as an app

Add Panchang to your home screen — it opens full-screen, like a native app, and needs no app store.

Android · Chrome
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top‑right corner
  2. Choose Add to Home screen
  3. Tap Add to confirm
iPhone · Safari
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) in the toolbar
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add in the top‑right corner
Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga and Karana are computed live from sun & moon positions and are approximate — accurate to within a tithi near transition times. For exact muhurat timing, confirm with a local panchang or pandit. Sunrise, sunset and Rahu Kaal use your device location when available.
